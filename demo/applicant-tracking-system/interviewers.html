Learned from last quarter's panels. Leniency: how much higher or lower than co-panelists they score everyone, taken out of their scores. Own-group gap: how much more they score candidates of their own group than their co-panelists do, compared with candidates of other groups. A clear gap flags them for a person to review and keeps them off panels meanwhile; it never changes any candidate's score.

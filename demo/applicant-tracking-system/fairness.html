Four-fifths check

Selection rate of group B divided by that of group A at each stage, this quarter. The rule works in both directions: the lower group's rate below 0.80 of the higher group's is the usual evidence of adverse impact. With a few dozen hires one quarter's ratio is noisy; the evaluation pools many companies. Group is collected voluntarily, kept apart from applications and only ever shown in aggregate. The same applicants are shown through two other processes for comparison.

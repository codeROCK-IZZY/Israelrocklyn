Contract text

SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Lumen HR Technologies Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Fees and Payment
1.1 Charges. The subscription fee is GBP 48k for each contract year. The Customer will settle undisputed invoices within thirty (30) calendar days.

2. General
2.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

3. Term
3.1 Duration. This contract is effective from 2026-04-22 ("Go-Live") and has a minimum commitment period of twelve (12) months. This is an evergreen arrangement: at the close of the minimum commitment period and of each extension, it is extended by one (1) year, except where the Customer has given written notice of cancellation no later than 90 days before that date.

4. Governing Law
4.1 Applicable law. The laws of Ireland govern this contract, and the Irish courts are the forum for any dispute.

5. General
5.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

6. Termination
6.1 The Customer may bring this contract to an end at its discretion, with or without reason, upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

7. Assignment
7.1 Any assignment or novation by the Supplier is void unless the Customer has consented to it in writing beforehand.

8. Limitation of Liability
8.1 The Supplier's aggregate liability arising out of this contract shall in no event exceed an amount equal to one and a half times the annual subscription fee.

9. Miscellaneous
9.1 The Supplier shall respond to Priority 1 incidents within four (4) hours and resolve them within twenty-four (24) hours.

10. Miscellaneous
10.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

Contract text

SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Northpoint Telecom Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Governing Law
1.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of Ireland, and the Irish courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

2. General
2.1 The Supplier shall respond to Priority 1 incidents within four (4) hours and resolve them within twenty-four (24) hours.

3. Termination
3.1 This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with this clause and not otherwise.

4. General
4.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

5. Assignment
5.1 The Supplier may freely assign or subcontract its obligations hereunder.

6. General
6.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

7. Fees and Payment
7.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £240,000 per annum. Invoices are payable thirty (30) days from receipt.

8. General
8.1 Notices shall be in writing and are deemed received two (2) business days after posting or thirty (30) minutes after sending by email.

9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 The aggregate liability of the Supplier in any Contract Year shall be limited to two times the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

10. General
10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

11. Term
11.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on May 19, 2026 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for thirty-six (36) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall be renewed automatically for further periods of 1 year each, save where a party serves notice to terminate not less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiry of the relevant period.

12. General
12.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £5,000,000 for the term.

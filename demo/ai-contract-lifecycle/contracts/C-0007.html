Contract text

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Kestrel Security Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Data Protection
1.1 Your data. When we handle personal information for you, we act as your processor under the data processing terms in Exhibit C.

2. General
2.1 The Supplier shall respond to Priority 1 incidents within four (4) hours and resolve them within twenty-four (24) hours.

3. Assignment
3.1 We may hand this contract over to another company in our group, or to a buyer of our business.

4. Limitation of Liability
4.1 If something goes wrong, the most we (the Supplier) will owe you is 150% of what you paid us over the previous twelve months.

5. General
5.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

6. Term
6.1 How long this lasts. You and we sign up on 6 March, 2025. The first period runs twenty-four (24) months from that date. After that it keeps going, 2 years at a time, until one of us says stop. To stop it, tell us in writing at least sixty (60) days before the current period ends.

7. General
7.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

8. Termination
8.1 You can cancel whenever you like, for any reason or none, by giving us thirty (30) days' notice.

9. Fees and Payment
9.1 What you pay. Your plan costs £150,000.00 per year. Please pay each invoice within 60 days.

10. General
10.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

11. Governing Law
11.1 Which law applies. This contract is under the law of Germany; any dispute will be heard by the courts of Munich.

12. Miscellaneous
12.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

13. Miscellaneous
13.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £5,000,000 for the term.

14. Miscellaneous
14.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

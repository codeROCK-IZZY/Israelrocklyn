Contract text

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Helix Cloud Systems Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Fees and Payment
1.1 Charges. The subscription fee is GBP 240k for each contract year. The Customer will settle undisputed invoices within 90 calendar days.

2. Term
2.1 Duration. This contract is effective from 13.02.2026 ("Go-Live") and has a minimum commitment period of 3 years. This is an evergreen arrangement: at the close of the minimum commitment period and of each extension, it is extended by twelve (12) months, except where the Customer has given written notice of cancellation no later than 90 days before that date.

3. General
3.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

4. Limitation of Liability
4.1 The parties agree that no limitation of liability applies to claims under this contract.

5. General
5.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £5,000,000 for the term.

6. Data Protection
6.1 Privacy. Each party will comply with applicable data protection legislation; the processor terms in Appendix 2 set out how the Supplier handles personal data on the Customer's behalf.

7. Termination
7.1 Save as expressly set out in this clause, this contract cannot be ended before the close of the minimum commitment period.

8. Governing Law
8.1 Applicable law. The laws of Delaware govern this contract, and the courts of Delaware are the forum for any dispute.

9. Assignment
9.1 Any assignment or novation by the Supplier is void unless the Customer has consented to it in writing beforehand.

10. Miscellaneous
10.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

11. Miscellaneous
11.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

12. Miscellaneous
12.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

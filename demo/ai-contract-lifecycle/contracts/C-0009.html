Contract text

SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Ashgrove Consulting LLP (the "Supplier").

1. Data Protection
1.1 The parties shall comply with the data processing terms annexed hereto, which form part of this Agreement and reflect Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

2. Termination
2.1 This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with this clause and not otherwise.

3. Fees and Payment
3.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £48,000 per annum. Invoices are payable 90 days from receipt.

4. General
4.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

5. Term
5.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on May 6, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for thirty-six (36) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall be renewed automatically for further periods of twelve (12) months each, save where a party serves notice to terminate not less than 90 days prior to the expiry of the relevant period.

6. Governing Law
6.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

7. General
7.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

8. Assignment
8.1 The Supplier shall not assign, novate or otherwise transfer any of its rights without the Customer's prior written approval.

9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 The aggregate liability of the Supplier in any Contract Year shall be limited to two times the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

10. General
10.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

11. Miscellaneous
11.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12. Miscellaneous
12.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

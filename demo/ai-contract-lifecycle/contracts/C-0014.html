STATEMENT OF WORK

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Kestrel Security Services Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Governing Law
1.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

2. General
2.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

3. Term
3.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on July 9, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for two (2) years. Thereafter, this Agreement shall be renewed automatically for further periods of one (1) year each, save where a party serves notice to terminate not less than 30 days prior to the expiry of the relevant period.

4. General
4.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

5. Fees and Payment
5.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £85,000 per annum. Invoices are payable sixty (60) days from receipt.

6. General
6.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

7. Limitation of Liability
7.1 The aggregate liability of the Supplier in any Contract Year shall be limited to one times the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

8. General
8.1 Notices shall be in writing and are deemed received two (2) business days after posting or thirty (30) minutes after sending by email.

9. Termination
9.1 This Agreement may be terminated only in accordance with this clause and not otherwise.

10. General
10.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

11. Assignment
11.1 The Supplier shall not assign, novate or otherwise transfer any of its rights without the Customer's prior written approval.

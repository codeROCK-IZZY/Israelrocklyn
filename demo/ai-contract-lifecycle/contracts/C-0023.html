Contract text

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Pinecrest Data GmbH (the "Supplier").

1. Term
1.1 How long this lasts. You and we sign up on 13 April, 2026. The first period runs three (3) years from that date. At the end of the first period it simply ends; to carry on we would sign a new order.

2. Termination
2.1 You cannot cancel early unless we seriously break this contract or go out of business.

3. General
3.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

4. Fees and Payment
4.1 What you pay. Your plan costs £85,000.00 per year. Please pay each invoice within thirty (30) days.

5. General
5.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £5,000,000 for the term.

6. Governing Law
6.1 Which law applies. This contract is under the law of Ireland; any dispute will be heard by the Irish courts.

7. Assignment
7.1 We will not hand this contract over to another company without first getting your written OK.

8. General
8.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 If something goes wrong, the most we (the Supplier) will owe you in total is £250,000.00.

10. General
10.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

Contract text

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Helix Cloud Systems Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Termination
1.1 The Customer shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving not less than 30 days' notice in writing.

2. Governing Law
2.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of Germany, and the courts of Munich shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

3. General
3.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

4. Data Protection
4.1 The parties shall comply with the data processing terms annexed hereto, which form part of this Agreement and reflect Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

5. Assignment
5.1 The Supplier shall not assign, novate or otherwise transfer any of its rights without the Customer's prior written approval.

6. General
6.1 Notices shall be in writing and are deemed received two (2) business days after posting or thirty (30) minutes after sending by email.

7. Fees and Payment
7.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £400,000 per annum. Invoices are payable fourteen (14) days from receipt.

8. Limitation of Liability
8.1 The Supplier's maximum aggregate liability shall be capped at the sum of £50,000.

9. General
9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

10. Term
10.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on 6th August 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for 36 months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall be renewed automatically for further periods of 1 year each, save where a party serves notice to terminate not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days prior to the expiry of the relevant period.

11. Miscellaneous
11.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12. Miscellaneous
12.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

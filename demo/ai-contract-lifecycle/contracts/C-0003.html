Contract text

SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Orbit Payments Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Governing Law
1.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of Delaware, and the courts of Delaware shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

2. General
2.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

3. Limitation of Liability
3.1 The Supplier's maximum aggregate liability shall be capped at the sum of £10,000.

4. General
4.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

5. Fees and Payment
5.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £150,000 per annum. Invoices are payable 30 days from receipt.

6. General
6.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

7. Assignment
7.1 The Supplier may freely assign or subcontract its obligations hereunder.

8. General
8.1 The Supplier shall respond to Priority 1 incidents within four (4) hours and resolve them within twenty-four (24) hours.

9. Term
9.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on 15th January 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for thirty-six (36) months. Thereafter, this Agreement shall be renewed automatically for further periods of 24 months each, save where a party serves notice to terminate not less than one hundred and eighty (180) days prior to the expiry of the relevant period.

10. Termination
10.1 The Customer shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving not less than 30 days' notice in writing.

11. Data Protection
11.1 The parties shall comply with the data processing terms annexed hereto, which form part of this Agreement and reflect Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

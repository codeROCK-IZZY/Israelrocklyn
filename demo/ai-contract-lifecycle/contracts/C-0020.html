Contract text

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Fernhill Facilities Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Assignment
1.1 Any assignment or novation by the Supplier is void unless the Customer has consented to it in writing beforehand.

2. General
2.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

3. Termination
3.1 The Customer may bring this contract to an end at its discretion, with or without reason, upon 60 days' prior written notice.

4. Data Protection
4.1 Privacy. Each party will comply with applicable data protection legislation; the processor terms in Appendix 2 set out how the Supplier handles personal data on the Customer's behalf.

5. General
5.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

6. Term
6.1 Duration. This contract is effective from 22 Jan 2026 ("Go-Live") and has a minimum commitment period of 2 years. This is an evergreen arrangement: at the close of the minimum commitment period and of each extension, it is extended by 24 months, except where the Customer has given written notice of cancellation no later than 30 days before that date.

7. General
7.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

8. Governing Law
8.1 Applicable law. The laws of Germany govern this contract, and the courts of Munich are the forum for any dispute.

9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 The Supplier's aggregate liability arising out of this contract shall in no event exceed an amount equal to two times the annual subscription fee.

10. General
10.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

11. Fees and Payment
11.1 Charges. The subscription fee is GBP 24k for each contract year. The Customer will settle undisputed invoices within fourteen (14) calendar days.

12. General
12.1 The Supplier shall respond to Priority 1 incidents within four (4) hours and resolve them within twenty-four (24) hours.

13. Miscellaneous
13.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

14. Miscellaneous
14.1 Notices shall be in writing and are deemed received two (2) business days after posting or thirty (30) minutes after sending by email.

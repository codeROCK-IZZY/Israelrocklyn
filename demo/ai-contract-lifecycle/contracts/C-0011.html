STATEMENT OF WORK

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Pinecrest Data GmbH (the "Supplier").

1. Limitation of Liability
1.1 The aggregate liability of the Supplier in any Contract Year shall be limited to one and a half times the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

2. Assignment
2.1 The Supplier may freely assign or subcontract its obligations hereunder.

3. Term
3.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on June 10, 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for 24 months. This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon expiry of that period, and the provisions on notice (90 days) apply only to early termination for cause.

4. General
4.1 Service credits of up to 10% of the monthly fees apply where availability falls below 99.5%.

5. Termination
5.1 The Customer shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving not less than ninety (90) days' notice in writing.

6. General
6.1 The Supplier shall maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least £5,000,000 for the term.

7. Governing Law
7.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

8. Fees and Payment
8.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £85,000 per annum. Invoices are payable 30 days from receipt.

9. General
9.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

10. Miscellaneous
10.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

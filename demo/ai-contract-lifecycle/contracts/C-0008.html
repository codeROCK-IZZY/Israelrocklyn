MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Between Northbridge Retail Ltd (the "Customer") and Quarry Analytics Ltd (the "Supplier").

1. Term
1.1 The term of this Agreement shall commence on 23rd April 2025 (the "Effective Date") and shall remain in force for thirty-six (36) months. This Agreement shall terminate automatically upon expiry of that period, and the provisions on notice (one hundred and eighty (180) days) apply only to early termination for cause.

2. General
2.1 Each party shall keep the other's Confidential Information secret for five (5) years after termination.

3. Governing Law
3.1 This Agreement and any dispute arising from it shall be construed in accordance with the law of Ireland, and the Irish courts shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

4. General
4.1 Any variation must be in writing and signed by both parties.

5. Limitation of Liability
5.1 The aggregate liability of the Supplier in any Contract Year shall be limited to one and a half times the Charges paid in that Contract Year.

6. Data Protection
6.1 The parties shall comply with the data processing terms annexed hereto, which form part of this Agreement and reflect Article 28 of the UK GDPR.

7. General
7.1 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice if the other commits a material breach which is not remedied within thirty (30) days.

8. Termination
8.1 The Customer shall be entitled to terminate this Agreement at any time without cause by giving not less than sixty (60) days' notice in writing.

9. General
9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.

10. Assignment
10.1 The Supplier shall not assign, novate or otherwise transfer any of its rights without the Customer's prior written approval.

11. Fees and Payment
11.1 In consideration of the Services the Customer shall pay £24,000 per annum. Invoices are payable forty-five (45) days from receipt.

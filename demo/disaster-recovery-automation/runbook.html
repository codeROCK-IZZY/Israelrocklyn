Runbooks into checked plans

Claude reads a wiki runbook and returns its steps (system, action, what each waits for, and the words it came from). The platform then checks the plan against the inventory: systems that exist, actions it can run, quotes that are really in the runbook, and no restore before the systems it depends on.

Example: the steps below were entered by hand from this runbook; Claude's output goes through the same checks.

Recovery runbook: payments platform (last edited two years ago)

1. Page the on-call DBA and post in #incident.
2. Restore api-07 from the latest backup in the recovery region.
3. Restore api-03 from its nightly backup.
4. Restore queue-01 and run the verification queries.
5. Restore ledger-legacy, which was retired last year.
6. Point the payments DNS record at the recovery region.
